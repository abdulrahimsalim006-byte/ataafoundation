WHO WE ARE

Giving with purpose. Serving with Mercy. Restoring Hope

Ataa Foundation is a student-led charitable organisation based in Kenya ,it is dedicated to supporting those in need and bringing hope to vulnerable individuals and communities

Through compassion, generosity and meaningful action, we aim to turn every act of giving into a lasting impact

Learn More About Us

OUR FOCUS

Small Acts. Meaningful Impact.

We focus on turning compassion into action by supporting people and communities where help is needed most.

Giving

Encouraging sincere generosity and making it easier to give towards meaningful causes.

Serving

Working together to support those in need through practical acts of service and compassion.

Hope

Helping create moments of dignity, relief and hope for individuals and communities.

FEATURED PROJECT

Making Giving Meaningful

At Ataa, we believe that giving is more than providing assistance. It is about restoring dignity, showing compassion, and reminding people that they are not forgotten.

Through student-led initiatives and community support, we work to turn generosity into meaningful action.
