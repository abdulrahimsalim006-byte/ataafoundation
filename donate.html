  2. Select Lipa na M-Pesa, then Paybill.
  3. Enter Paybill number 985050.
  4. Enter account number 0111439302.
  5. Enter your donation amount and carefully review the displayed recipient details.
  6. Confirm only if the details are correct.
Please note: Confirm the account number and recipient details before sending money. Do not complete the transaction if the details are unclear.
