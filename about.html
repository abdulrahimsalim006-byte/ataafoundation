ABOUT ATAA

Who We Are

A student-led charitable organisation committed to giving with purpose, serving with mercy, and bringing hope to those in need.

OUR STORY

Giving With Purpose

Ataa Foundation is a student-led charitable organisation based in Kenya, created with a desire to turn compassion into real impact.

We believe that giving is not only about providing material support. It is also about showing compassion, preserving dignity, and treating every person with the care they deserve.

Through every act of giving, we seek to ease burdens and bring relief where it is needed most. And with mercy at the heart of our service, we hope to leave behind something more — a sense of hope.

OUR DIRECTION

What Guides Ataa

Our work is shaped by a simple commitment to serve people with sincerity, compassion, and purpose.

Our Mission

To turn compassion into meaningful action by supporting people and communities in need through sincere giving, practical service, and initiatives that bring hope.

Our Vision

To build a culture of purposeful giving and compassionate service where acts of kindness create meaningful and lasting hope.

OUR VALUES

What We Stand For

The principles behind how we give, serve, and work with others.

Giving

We encourage generosity that is sincere, purposeful, and directed towards meaningful needs.

Mercy

We seek to serve others with compassion, understanding, and respect for their dignity.

Hope

We believe every act of kindness can bring relief, encouragement, and hope to someone in need.

Service

We believe meaningful change requires action, responsibility, and a willingness to serve.

HOW WE SERVE

Our approach focuses on understanding needs, working together, and using available resources responsibly.
